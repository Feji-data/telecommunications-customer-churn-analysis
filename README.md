# Telecommunications Customer Churn Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

Customer retention is an important factor in the success of any telecommunications company. This project analyzes customer churn data to identify patterns and factors associated with customers leaving the company.

The analysis covers **7,043 customers**, of which **1,869 customers churned**, resulting in an overall churn rate of **26.54%**.

The objective of this project is to transform customer data into meaningful business insights that can help the company understand customer churn and improve retention strategies.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis were to:

* Analyze the overall customer churn rate.
* Identify customer groups with higher churn rates.
* Investigate how contract type affects customer churn.
* Analyze churn across different internet service types.
* Examine the relationship between customer tenure and churn.
* Identify the impact of additional services such as online security, online backup, and device protection.
* Analyze churn patterns across different payment methods.
* Provide actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. Overall Churn

The dataset contains **7,043 customers**.

* **5,174 customers** remained with the company.
* **1,869 customers** churned.
* The overall churn rate was **26.54%**.

This indicates that more than one-quarter of the customers in the dataset had left the company.

### 2. Partner Status

Single customers recorded a higher churn rate compared with customers who had partners. Single customers also had the lowest number of retained customers within the partner analysis.

### 3. Customer Tenure

Customers with shorter tenure recorded significantly higher churn. The **0–9 tenure group** recorded the highest churn rate, with **923 customers churning compared with 931 customers retained**.

This suggests that newer customers are more vulnerable to churn.

### 4. Internet Service

**Fiber Optic** customers recorded the highest churn rate at approximately **41.9%**.

This indicates that Fiber Optic customers require particular attention when developing customer retention strategies.

### 5. Online Security

Customers who did not have **Online Security** showed considerably higher churn compared with customers who subscribed to the service.

### 6. Online Backup

Customers without **Online Backup** recorded higher churn, with **1,233 customers churning compared with 1,855 customers retained**.

### 7. Device Protection

Customers without **Device Protection** also recorded higher churn, with **1,211 customers churning compared with 1,884 customers retained**.

### 8. Contract Type

Customers on **Month-to-Month contracts** recorded significantly higher churn compared with customers on longer-term contracts.

There were **1,655 churned customers** on month-to-month contracts compared with **2,220 customers who remained**.

### 9. Payment Method

Customers using **Electronic Check** recorded high churn, with **1,071 customers churning compared with 1,294 customers retained**.

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations can help improve customer retention:

### 1. Focus on New Customers

The company should strengthen onboarding and early-stage customer engagement because customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn.

### 2. Encourage Long-Term Contracts

The company should introduce incentives such as discounts, loyalty benefits, or additional services to encourage month-to-month customers to move to longer-term contracts.

### 3. Investigate Fiber Optic Customer Experience

Since Fiber Optic customers recorded a high churn rate, the company should investigate service quality, pricing, customer support, and customer expectations within this segment.

### 4. Promote Value-Added Services

The company should encourage customers to subscribe to services such as Online Security, Online Backup, and Device Protection through bundles, targeted offers, or loyalty incentives.

### 5. Review Electronic Check Customers

The company should investigate why customers using Electronic Check experience higher churn and consider promoting alternative payment methods where appropriate.

### 6. Develop Targeted Retention Strategies

Instead of treating all customers the same, the company should identify high-risk customer segments and provide targeted retention campaigns based on their characteristics and behavior.

---

## 📌 Conclusion

This analysis shows that customer churn is not evenly distributed across the customer base. Certain groups, particularly newer customers, month-to-month customers, Fiber Optic users, customers without additional protection services, and Electronic Check users, demonstrate higher levels of churn.

By identifying these high-risk segments, the company can develop more targeted retention strategies, improve customer experience, and potentially reduce customer attrition.
